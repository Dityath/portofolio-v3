Audience/job: founders, product and engineering leads deciding in a few minutes whether to talk to Ditya.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is one continuous thought you scroll through, not a stack of sections; it refuses hero + text blocks + card list.

OWN-WORLD: Graphite #0F1115 ground, 1px hairlines, square corners; blueprint blue #4C8DFF for build/interactive; amber #E5B25D only for drawn annotations; Inter Tight for words, Plex Mono only for data and labels; the Dionysus bust as the single 3D object, always front-facing (yaw within ±35°).

STORY: Visitor meets Ditya (name + role, one line), watches the bust being framed, constrained, decided and built (one word + one line per move), swipes through three projects as large visuals with one question each, sees the path Design → Build → Lead drawn as a line, and ends at one sentence and an email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: two-line solid/outline role headline, one short line, two actions. Right/center: the front-facing bust with amber leaders anchored to points projected from the 3D model. A scroll cue at the bottom.

FORM: Pinned scroll-scrubbed story (sticky stage, progress-driven chapters), horizontal pinned work rail, scroll-drawn path. Owner-specified form; no concept-seed roll (owner pinned world and form). Signature interaction: scroll scrubs the bust through the four moves and dissolves it into points.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
